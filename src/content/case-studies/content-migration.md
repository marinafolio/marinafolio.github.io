- Consolidated approximately 6,000 English-language help articles into 382 maintainable guides.

- Reduced duplication and content fragmentation across the help center.

- Maintained strong user feedback during the migration, averaging 150–200 daily upvotes with a 2.5:1 positive sentiment ratio.

- Established a scalable structure for future documentation governance and updates.

## Overview

As part of a larger migration from a legacy help system to Zendesk, I led the planning and consolidation work for a large-scale help center restructuring initiative.

Over time, the documentation for a global edtech platform had grown bloated and inconsistent, with thousands of duplicative or overlapping guides spread across product areas. Rather than migrate content as-is, I developed a structured approach to auditing, consolidating, and reorganizing the help center to improve usability, consistency, and long-term maintainability.

## The challenge

The migration required consolidating thousands of guides while maintaining consistency across product areas and meeting a fixed launch timeline.

Internally, the lack of a shared audit framework or tracking system meant writers were making isolated decisions, increasing the risk of inconsistent guidance, duplicated work, and delayed progress.

The work also required making consolidation decisions without complete usage data for every guide, balancing speed, usability, and long-term maintainability throughout the migration process.

## My approach

I approached the migration by establishing a clear, repeatable structure for auditing and consolidating content.

- I began by auditing a subset of integration guidance to identify duplication, content gaps, and overlapping workflows. This established a repeatable framework for evaluating and consolidating content across product areas.

## Outcomes

- The tracking system quickly became the team’s single source of truth, giving writers shared visibility into ownership, scope, and progress while reducing back-and-forth across product areas.

- To support a smooth transition, I created a comprehensive old → new link map so Customer Support could update references confidently and direct users without confusion.

- With duplication removed and structure simplified, the help site became easier to navigate and significantly lighter to maintain.

- Audit data provided clear visibility into progress and remaining work during the migration.

The migration ultimately transformed the help center from a fragmented collection of legacy content into a more scalable, maintainable documentation system with clearer ownership, improved usability, and stronger operational visibility.
