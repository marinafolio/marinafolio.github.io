## The challenge

The new product introduced differences across administrator, instructor, and student workflows, requiring documentation that was both role-specific and consistent in structure.

Without clear and reliable guidance, users risked confusion during the transition, especially when familiar actions behaved differently in the updated system.

The documentation needed to:

- Clearly explain updated workflows without assuming prior knowledge of the new system 

- Maintain consistency across 22 guides while accounting for differences between user roles  

- Accurately reflect real product behavior, including edge cases and variations in user flows  


## My approach

I approached the documentation by establishing a clear, repeatable structure that could scale across guides while remaining easy for users to follow.

To understand where guidance was needed, I worked directly in the product and completed key workflows for each user role. This helped identify differences between administrator, instructor, and student experiences, as well as areas where users might encounter confusion during the transition.

I created a consistent framework for each guide using task-based headings, step-by-step instructions, and supporting context where necessary. 

To manage the documentation set at scale, I organized guides by user role and tracked content through drafting, staging, and publication workflows to maintain consistency across all 22 guides.

As I documented workflows, I validated instructions against real product behavior to identify gaps, inconsistencies, and edge cases requiring clarification. I also used screenshots selectively to support more complex steps, ensuring visuals added clarity without overwhelming the content.

To maintain consistency across all 22 guides, I applied the same patterns for structure, terminology, formatting, and instructional language throughout the documentation set.

## Outcomes

- Delivered 22 structured, role-based guides that provided clear, task-focused support for administrators, instructors, and students.

- Made it easier for users to understand and adopt the updated product by providing consistent, step-by-step guidance across workflows.

- Improved the accuracy and reliability of documentation by validating instructions against real product behavior and identifying issues during the writing process.

- Established a consistent structure and set of patterns that supported maintainability as the product and documentation evolved.
