<h1 class="sample-title">Understanding your instructor's feedback</h1>

After your assignment is graded, your instructor’s feedback helps you understand what you did well and where you can improve. This guide explains how to access your feedback and use it to improve your work.

## Accessing your feedback

To view your feedback, open your assignment and select the **similarity score** to open your graded submission. Then, select the **Feedback** tab at the top of your screen.

Your instructor’s feedback appears in different areas of your submission. Each type of feedback highlights something different, from specific edits to overall comments. Reviewing all of these areas will give you a complete picture of your work and how to improve it.


## On-submission feedback

On your submission, you’ll see different types of marks and annotations that indicate your instructor’s feedback.


### Highlighted text

Highlighted text shows where your instructor has left feedback on a specific part of your work.

/>

Select the highlighted text to open the related feedback in the margin.

<img

/>

Highlights may appear in one of three colors. These colors do not have a set meaning unless your instructor has chosen to use them that way.


### Strikethrough text

Strikethrough text indicates content your instructor suggests removing or revising.

/>

### Inline comments

Inline comments are brief notes added directly to your submission. These appear as text within your paper.

### QuickMark and comment icons

You may also see icons on your submission that indicate feedback.

- A comment icon <img 
  src="/images/writing-samples/rf-05.png" 
  alt="Comment icon on a submission indicating instructor feedback." 
  class="inline-image" 
/> indicates that your instructor has left a comment. Select the icon to open the corresponding feedback in the margin.

- A QuickMark icon <img 
  src="/images/writing-samples/rf-06.png" 
  alt="QuickMark icon on a submission indicating instructor feedback." 
  class="inline-image" 
/>  indicates reusable feedback your instructor uses across assignments. Select the icon to open the corresponding feedback in the margin.


## In-margin feedback

The margin of your submission is where you’ll find detailed, actionable feedback from your instructor.

Select any margin card to highlight the part of your submission that the feedback refers to.

<img 
  src="/images/writing-samples/rf-07.png" 
  alt="Feedback cards in the margin of a submission indicating instructor feedback." 
  class="sample-image" 
/>

Feedback with a pin icon indicates that it has been prioritized by your instructor. You can also view pinned feedback in the Summary tab.

Some feedback may be linked to a specific criterion in your rubric. This is indicated by a link icon next to the criterion name <img 
  src="/images/writing-samples/rf-08.png" 
  alt="Summary comment in feedback sidebar of a submission." 
  class="inline-image" 
/>.


## Summary feedback

To view summary feedback, select the **Summary** tab in the feedback side panel.

The summary comment includes your instructor’s overall feedback on the quality of your paper.

<img 
  src="/images/writing-samples/rf-09.png" 
  alt="Summary comment in feedback sidebar of a submission." 
  class="sample-image" 
/>

## Using your feedback

Once you’ve reviewed everything, look for patterns in the feedback. Focusing on repeated suggestions can help you make meaningful improvements in your next assignment.
